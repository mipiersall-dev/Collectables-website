The Kingdom & Republic of Romania

An aggregated specialized study of Romanian military awards, civilian merit decorations, and chivalric national orders. This inventory tracks the technical craftsmanship and historical evolution of phaleristic insignia spanning from the classic 19th-century foundation under King Carol I, through the complex wartime battlefront re-issuances of the First and Second World Wars, and concluding with the distinctive badge variations crafted during the mid-20th century Communist state era.
