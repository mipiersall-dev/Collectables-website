The Instrument Vault

A curated survey of historical acoustic luthiery, classical string designs, and legacy instrumentation. Beyond their technical engineering, these objects represent a living generational bond—built from a family's shared love of music, where father and son grew up playing together on these very instruments. This archive preserves both the physical craftsmanship of these legacy stringed art forms and the timeless resonance of the songs passed down between them.
