Phaleristics Collection

An authoritative historical registry dedicated to the study of international orders of chivalry, military bravery decorations, and historical campaign medals. This archive documents certified design metrics, strike metal composition, and official suspension ribbons, tracking changes across historical mintage periods. Each specimen balances technical database indexing records against clean photographic close-ups to preserve fine engravings, enamel work, and surface patina natively.
